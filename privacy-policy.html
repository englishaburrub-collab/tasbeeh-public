Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Tasbeeh app for Android and iPhone and this public website.

Data stored on your device

Tasbeeh stores your collection, including the names, phrases, targets, colors, and counts you enter. It also stores completed rounds, daily activity history, and app settings. The app uses this data to run your counters, show your history, and apply your preferences. The app stores the data in a local database on your device and does not send it to the Tasbeeh developer.

Google user data

On Android, you can choose to sign in with Google to back up and restore Tasbeeh data. Tasbeeh uses your Google account to authorize access and display the connected email address. It requests access only to Tasbeeh’s private Google Drive app data folder. When you choose to back up, Tasbeeh sends your collection, counts, daily history, and settings to that folder. When you restore, Tasbeeh reads the latest backup from that folder. The app contains no advertising or analytics.

Reminders

If you enable reminders, Tasbeeh uses your device’s notification permission and local time zone to schedule notifications on your device. You can turn reminders off in Tasbeeh or manage notification permissions in your device settings.

Backup files and sharing

If you export a local backup, Tasbeeh creates a readable JSON file containing your collection, counts, daily history, and settings. Your device’s file picker lets you choose where the file is saved or which file to restore. If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy. Tasbeeh uploads a Google Drive backup only when you choose to back up. Anyone you share an exported file with can read its contents.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains in the local app database while you use Tasbeeh. Deleting or resetting a counter preserves recorded daily activity. To remove all data from the current installation, clear the app’s storage where your device offers that option or delete the app and its local data. Exported copies and Google Drive backups are stored separately. Delete exported copies from the locations where you saved them. To delete Google Drive backups, use Google Drive’s settings to delete Tasbeeh’s hidden app data.

Website and support

These pages do not use analytics, tracking cookies, or forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.
