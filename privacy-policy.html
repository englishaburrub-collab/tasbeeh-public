Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Tasbeeh app for Android and iPhone and this public website.

Data stored on your device

Tasbeeh stores your collection, including the names, phrases, targets, colors, and counts you enter. It also stores completed rounds, daily activity history, and app settings. The app uses this data to run your counters, show your history, and apply your preferences. The app stores the data in a local database on your device and does not send it to the Tasbeeh developer.

Google user data

Tasbeeh has no accounts and does not use Sign in with Google or connect to Google Drive through the app. It does not request access to your Google account or use Google APIs to collect, use, store, or share Google user data. The app contains no advertising or analytics.

Reminders

If you enable reminders, Tasbeeh uses your device’s notification permission and local time zone to schedule notifications on your device. You can turn reminders off in Tasbeeh or manage notification permissions in your device settings.

Backup files and sharing

If you export a backup, Tasbeeh creates a readable JSON file containing your collection, counts, daily history, and settings. Your device’s file picker lets you choose where the file is saved or which file to restore. If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy. Tasbeeh does not automatically upload or sync your collection. Anyone you share a backup with can read its contents.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains in the local app database while you use Tasbeeh. Deleting or resetting a counter preserves recorded daily activity. To remove all data from the current installation, clear the app’s storage where your device offers that option or delete the app and its local data. Exported copies are stored separately. Delete those copies from the locations where you saved them, including any cloud storage you chose.

Website and support

These pages do not use analytics, tracking cookies, or forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

For general support or questions about this policy, use the Tasbeeh support page on GitHub. Issues are public. Keep backup files and personal information out of your posts.
