Tasbeeh

Tasbeeh is an offline dhikr counter for Android and iPhone. Create counters with your own phrases and targets, tap to record each repetition, and use Lap mode to track completed rounds. You can review your daily and monthly activity, set reminders, and export a local backup.

Optional Google Drive backup on Android

In Settings, you can sign in with Google to back up and restore your Tasbeeh data. Tasbeeh uses your Google account to authorize access and display the connected email address. Drive access is limited to Tasbeeh’s private app data folder.

When you choose Back up to Google Drive, Tasbeeh saves your counters, counts, daily history, and settings to that folder. When you choose Restore from Google Drive, Tasbeeh reads the latest backup and asks before replacing local data. Counting, reminders, and local backups work offline.
