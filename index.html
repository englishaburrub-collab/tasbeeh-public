Features

Custom counters and Lap mode

Create counters with your own phrases, targets, and colors. Tap to count, and use Lap mode to repeat a target and track completed rounds.

History and reminders

Review daily activity and monthly totals. Set a local reminder or choose a time to reset your counters each day.

Duaas and prayer log

Save and search duaas for offline reading. Record obligatory and voluntary prayers and review weekly and monthly summaries.

Local and Google Drive backups

Export and restore a JSON backup. On supported builds, you can connect Google Drive to sync your workspace and keep named restore points in the app’s private Drive folder.
