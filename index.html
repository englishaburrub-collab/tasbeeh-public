Tasbeeh

An offline counter for your daily dhikr. Create a counter with your own phrase and target, then tap the dial to count each repetition.

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ 24 of 33

Count at your own pace

Keep a collection of phrases with individual goals and colors. Use Lap mode to repeat a target and record completed rounds.

Build a daily routine

See daily activity and monthly totals. Set an optional reminder and choose when your daily counts reset. The app is available in English and Arabic.

Keep your data on your device

Use Tasbeeh without signing in. Export a backup file when you want to keep a copy or restore your collection on another device.
